Traffic & Emissions

Street layouts tested against counted traffic

The map opens on current surface-street speeds and the slow approaches that connect. Pick an intersection and the agent drafts street layouts, then tests each one against a verified turning count.

  • Hard gates run first, then modeled CO₂ decides the order.
  • Every evaluated design stays open to inspect, rejected ones included.
  • Name an alternative to revise and the rest of your brief is kept.

Stormwater

Rain gardens sited where the land supports them

The map opens on terrain screening, with rainfall, drainage and property-use layers. Pick a site and the agent places a rain garden footprint from aerial outlines and property context, with up to three alternatives inside it.

  • Ask for a different size, depth, shape or placement.
  • Storage is calculated while drainage stays marked unknown.
  • Runoff what-ifs use the source areas, coefficient and storm you set.

Sourced data, stated limits

What it is built on

Vexcel
Aerial imagery and building outlines
CoStar
Property use, units, parking and floor area
LADOT
Turning counts at signalised intersections
USGS
Bare-earth terrain at 1 m
LA County
Subbasins and design rainfall

What it does not claim

  • Live speeds help you find a place. Comparisons need a verified turning count.
  • No traffic recommendation has been established yet.
  • A terrain path is an inference, not a flood-depth map or a verified inlet connection.
  • A reviewed concept cites its facts. It does not establish physical feasibility.
  • Missing data is reported as missing, never as zero.

Runs locally, with your own keys

Projects, inputs and results stay in local storage on your machine. Start with a focus and one place.

HOW THE GARDEN WORKS

A planted place for rain

Curb openings bring water into a planted depression. Soil and gravel form the conceptual section; an overflow route carries water beyond ponding capacity.

Source & design basis＋

Vexcel building outlines, imagery collection dated July 3, 2026, anchor the site geometry. CoStar supplies the property identity, 28 homes and three-story context.

This is an authored demonstration concept. Facades, plants, ground materials and subsurface layers are illustrative. The model is an architectural reconstruction, not a Gaussian splat or surveyed construction model.

The two garden cells assume 150 mm of effective ponding. The rainfall control is a storage calculation, not a hydraulic simulation. Connected drainage, soil infiltration, utilities and any proposed outlet require verification; no infiltration benefit is counted.

The proposed street-edge treatment requires right-of-way and access review. The concept keeps an entrance gap, but existing curb cuts, dimensions and construction feasibility need field confirmation.

An early design to inspect, discuss and refine.

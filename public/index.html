                Sources, methods & limitations

                Historical turning demand drives this simulation. The current traffic snapshot helps identify an opportunity; its speed and slowdown observations are not turning volumes, recurring congestion or measured emissions.

                Emissions cover one hour of offered traffic, including a 15-minute clearance period. Completion is measured after clearance. A lower total cannot justify stranded traffic or a failed hard gate.

                Detection candidates require field verification; an empty response does not confirm that a crossing or driveway is absent. Mapped checks do not establish ownership, access approval or construction feasibility.

                To revise a design, describe a change to its approach, length or lane allocation in the brief, or add a keep-clear marking, then continue the project. The next run evaluates one bounded comparison under the same selection policy.

                Delay includes waiting to enter; unfinished trips contribute a lower bound. Queue is the maximum simultaneous total along an approach. Noise is the highest hourly mean source level across approach edges and paired runs. Roadway noise is a modeled source level, not exposure at a building. A 10% noise-energy increase is about 0.414 dB; percentages are not applied directly to decibels.
